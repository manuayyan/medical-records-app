## Data Structure Notes
- Patient demographics (Name, Age, Sex)
- Visit details (Date/Time of ED arrival)
- Vital signs (PR, SpO2, SBP, DBP, RR, AVPU scale, Pain scale)
- Triage information (ESI level, Triaged to, Retriaged To)
- Medical details (Complaints, Diagnosis, Disposition)
- Special timing metrics (Door To ECG, Door to Needle)
- Other clinical data (POCUS, Mobility)

## Requirements
- Access for Medical Records staff and admin only
- Dynamic data capture from Google Sheet
- 1-week data retention with auto-deletion
- Daily data updates
